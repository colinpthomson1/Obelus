# Naming and Trademark Note

This document is a practical design-team warning, not legal advice or trademark clearance.

## Material collision risk

Several existing software and technology businesses use the name Obelus, including:

- [obelus.com](https://obelus.com/), an active software, app-development, and digital-design business.
- [obelus.4gentic.ai](https://obelus.4gentic.ai/), a paper-review product using the closely related idea “mark what you doubt.”
- [obelus.in](https://obelus.in/), a real-time cybersecurity platform.
- Additional OBELUS registrations appear in third-party trademark databases across multiple jurisdictions and classes.

Because the proposed product is also software and the historic meaning is central to more than one existing product, formal clearance should happen before public launch.

## Recommended clearance scope

Ask qualified counsel to review:

1. Word-mark availability in every intended launch market.
2. Relevant software, AI, transcription, research, media, and information-service classes.
3. Confusingly similar marks, not only exact matches.
4. Domain-name and social-handle availability.
5. App-store names and marketplace listings.
6. The final symbol for similarity to registered device marks.
7. The master and campaign taglines.

## Package status

The brand package is ready for internal product design, prototyping, testing, and stakeholder review. Treat external launch use as pending professional clearance.

If the name changes, the underlying strategic and visual system can be adapted: live verification, constructive skepticism, the evidence-line device, claim-state colors, typography, motion, and layouts do not depend entirely on the word mark.
