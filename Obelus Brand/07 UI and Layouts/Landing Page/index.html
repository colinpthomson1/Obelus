01 / THE PRINCIPLE

Question claims.
Not people.

Obelus examines what was said without assigning motive to the speaker. It shows what supports a claim, what conflicts with it, and what remains unresolved.

Stay in the conversation.

The research happens alongside the transcript, so evidence arrives before the moment for a better follow-up or decision has passed.

01

Listen

Obelus follows a consented live transcript from an in-person or remote conversation.

02

Notice

Checkable claims are surfaced automatically, or selected directly by anyone in the room.

03

Examine

Reliable sources are compared while provisional status, conflicts, and freshness remain visible.

04

Respond

Ask the next question, correct the premise, or leave with a sourced record of the conversation.
