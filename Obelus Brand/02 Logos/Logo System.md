# Obelus Logo System

## The Dialogue Axis

The master mark is a custom obelus. Its dots sit on opposite sides of the center axis, suggesting two participants in a conversation. The horizontal stroke is the claim under review. A soft editorial taper gives the stroke direction without turning it into an aggressive arrow.

The form remains recognizable as an obelus while moving beyond a typed division glyph. That distinction matters: the bare symbol is generic, while the custom geometry, wordmark, color, motion, and product language create a coherent identity.

## Meaning

- Upper and lower dots: independent voices, checks, or sources
- Horizontal stroke: a spoken claim placed under examination
- Offset axis: exchange, dialogue, and a live process
- Soft terminal: inquiry moving toward context, never accusation

These meanings are contemporary brand metaphors, not historical claims about the ancient mark.

## Primary configuration

Use the horizontal lockup whenever space permits. Use the symbol alone for application icons, favicons, compact navigation, status indicators, and loading motion. Use the stacked lockup for square or centered compositions.

## Color hierarchy

1. Evidence Blue symbol + Obelus Ink wordmark on Paper or Cloud
2. Cloud single-color reverse on Obelus Ink or Evidence Blue
3. Obelus Ink single-color for restrained applications
4. Pure black or white only when a production process requires absolute monochrome

Do not place the full-color lockup on photography or uncontrolled backgrounds.

## Clear space

The minimum clear space on every side is 1.5 times the diameter of either symbol dot. No type, rule, image edge, or interactive control may enter this area.

## Minimum size

- Symbol: 16 px digital or 5 mm print
- Horizontal lockup: 96 px digital or 25 mm print
- Below 20 px, use the micro symbol with a fully rounded bar
- Do not use the tapered display bar when rasterized below 20 px

## Alignment

Align the lockup optically, not by the transparent SVG bounds. In interface headers, align the wordmark baseline with adjacent navigation text. In centered layouts, center the visible symbol, not the 64-unit construction frame.

## Do not

- Type the Unicode division glyph and call it the logo.
- Re-center the dots or alter their offset.
- Rotate, skew, outline, or add effects to the mark.
- Use verdict red or supported green as default logo colors.
- Put the symbol inside an arbitrary circle or shield.
- Replace the first letter of the wordmark with the symbol.
- Stretch the mark or change the symbol-to-wordmark relationship.
- Animate the mark with shaking, bouncing, flashing, or alarm behavior.

## Naming and pronunciation

Write the company name as **Obelus** in text and pronounce it **OB-uh-lus**. Do not use “Diabolos”; it is unrelated to the symbol and introduces an ominous meaning the brand explicitly avoids.
