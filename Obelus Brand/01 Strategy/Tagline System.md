# Tagline System

Obelus uses a small messaging system instead of forcing one line to serve every context.

## Primary lines

### Master tagline

**Evidence at conversation speed.**

Use for the homepage, product introductions, decks, and category-building communications. It communicates both evidence and the real-time advantage without promising infallibility.

### Expressive campaign line

**See what stands up.**

Use for launch campaigns, editorial layouts, social creative, and feature stories. It connects scrutiny with evidence while leaving room for curiosity.

### Purpose line

**Keep the conversation honest.**

Use selectively in mission-led communications. Avoid using it beside individual speakers because it can imply suspected deception.

### Category descriptor

**Live verification for spoken conversations.**

Use whenever the audience may not yet understand the product.

## Selection guidance

- Product and corporate: Evidence at conversation speed.
- Editorial or launch: See what stands up.
- Philosophy and thought leadership: Every claim deserves context.
- Demonstration call to action: Bring evidence into the room.
- Consumer-facing campaign: Do not just hear it. Check it.
- Feature messaging: Research that keeps up.

These lines are creative recommendations, not trademark-cleared marks.
