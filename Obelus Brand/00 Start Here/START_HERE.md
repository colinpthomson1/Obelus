# Obelus Brand

Version 1.0 - August 2026

Obelus is a real-time verification layer for spoken conversations. The identity is built around constructive skepticism: fast enough to help in the moment, transparent enough to trust afterward, and calm enough to belong in the room.

## Brand at a glance

- Category: Live verification for spoken conversations
- Master tagline: Evidence at conversation speed.
- Expressive line: See what stands up.
- Purpose line: Keep the conversation honest.
- Brand essence: Constructive skepticism
- Core idea: Not a verdict. A reason to look closer.
- Primary typeface: Instrument Sans Variable, licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1

## Important launch note

This package is a complete design system, not legal clearance. Existing software and technology businesses use the name Obelus. Read `10 Legal and Licenses/Naming and Trademark Note.md` and commission a professional trademark, domain, and social-handle search before a public launch or substantial media investment.

## Package principles

- Use evidence, not verdicts.
- Question claims, not people.
- Make provisional findings visibly provisional.
- Pair every status color with a label and icon.
- Keep the mark calm and composed; never turn it into a warning siren.
- Show sources, dates, confidence, and corrections as part of the experience.

All final deliverables live inside this folder. The `99 Source` directory contains reproducible build and verification tooling.
